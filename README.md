# Benedikt Eickhoff

**Writer · artist · systems designer**  
Berlin · Founder of [Fountain Coach](https://fountain.coach)

I work across dramaturgy, literature, music and software. The question connecting these fields is simple:

**What happens when meaning becomes executable?**

My starting point is often a text, a scene, a score or a relationship between people. I develop forms in which those things can be read, performed, transformed and published.

## How I work

I conceive and direct software systems; I do not hand-write their implementation code. Language models and software agents do that work under my direction.

I define purposes, domain boundaries, scenarios and acceptance conditions, and evaluate the results.

This is **AI-directed systems design**, not a claim of personal authorship of every line of code. It brings editorial judgment and dramaturgical thinking into software production while keeping authority and responsibility explicit.

## Selected work

- **[Teatro](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/Teatro)** — tools for scores, scripts, rendering and performance.
- **[MIDI 2.0](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/midi2)** — machine-readable musical communication and interoperability across software environments.
- **[FountainAuthKit](https://github.com/Fountain-Coach/FountainAuthKit)** — a foundation for writer-controlled publishing domains and explicit authorization.
- **Reframe** — an evolving approach to reading, interpretation and author-controlled publishing.

These are parts of an ongoing practice, not a list of products claimed to be finished.
